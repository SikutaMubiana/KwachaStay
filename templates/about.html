About KwachaStay

KwachaStay is a Zambian property marketplace connecting property seekers, landlords, real estate agents, property managers, hotels and accommodation providers across multiple major cities in Zambia.

Discover houses, apartments, hostels, hotels, villas and short-term accommodation across Zambia’s major cities — all in one transparent, easy-to-use digital platform.

Our platform serves students, tenants, families, professionals, travellers, tourists, landlords, real estate agents, property managers, and hospitality businesses operating across our launch cities.
